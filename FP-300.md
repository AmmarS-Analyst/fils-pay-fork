# FP-300: the missing feature is Refunds

The previous developer started refunds (FP-212, "v1.9") and never finished them. `POST /v1/refunds` answered **501**, the helpers had money bugs (BUGS.md #11–14), and nothing downstream knew refunds existed. The repo comments called the remaining work "half a day" ("just wire it"). It wasn't: the refund touched the API, ledger, settlement, webhooks, reporting, reconciliation and the dashboard.

## Money flow (one example, MDR 2.5%)

Capture 10.000 BD, then refund 4.000 BD:

| Step | merchant_payable | platform_fee_revenue | Payout effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capture | +10.000 gross, −0.250 fee | +0.250 | batch gross +10.000, fee +0.250 |
| Refund | −4.000 gross, +0.100 fee back | −0.100 | batch gross −4.000, fee −0.100 |
| **Net** | **5.850** | 0.150 | **payout 5.850** |

After payout, `merchant_payable` is exactly 0 (Go test `TestCloseBatch_NetsRefundsIntoPayout`).

## Decisions

- **Two paths, one implementation.** `/v1/refunds` is for API-key integrations. The merchant-scoped path matches the dashboard, payment links and webhooks.
- **Idempotency key comes from the client.** The draft handler generated one server-side (`crypto.randomUUID()`), which makes every network retry a new refund.
- **Carry-forward, not negative payouts.** `ledger_entries` forbids non-positive amounts and a negative bank payout isn't possible, so a refund larger than the day's net waits for the next day with captures. It's never lost, and the ledger already reflects it.
- **Status rule left as finance decided.** A partial capture fully refunded keeps its capture status, so the remaining authorization stays usable (`refundState.ts`, finance decision of 21/07).

## Bugs found while building (also in BUGS.md)

- #27 Capture and void had no server-side role check (support users and admin impersonation could move money).
- #28 `bhd()` crashed the dashboard on any negative amount (every refund event).
- #29 `refund_merchant_summary` joined on the refund's own id and was always empty. The reporting refund columns didn't exist.
- #30 Reconciliation claimed to count refunds in "pending" and didn't.
